Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 July 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the iShabbat app ("the app") handles information. The app does not require an account and does not ask you to provide personal details such as your name or email.

Location

If you choose "Use My Location," the app reads your device location on the device to calculate accurate Shabbat candle-lighting and Havdalah times for your area. Your location is used only for this calculation. It is sent to the Hebcal service (see below) as approximate coordinates to retrieve times, and is not stored by the app or used to identify you. You can instead choose a city manually and skip location access entirely.

Shabbat times (Hebcal)

The app retrieves Shabbat times from the Hebcal API. Requests include the location or city needed to compute times. Please refer to Hebcal's own privacy practices for how they handle requests.

Advertising

The app displays ads through Google AdMob. To support ads, Google's SDK may collect device identifiers and advertising data. On first launch the app asks, via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, whether you allow tracking:

  • If you allow tracking, you may receive personalized ads.
  • If you decline, ads are limited to non-personalized ones.

Where required by law, a consent form (via Google's User Messaging Platform) is shown before ads load. For details on data collected by Google, see the Google Privacy Policy.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. The app does not run a server and does not send push notifications from the network.

Data we do not collect

The app does not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, or health data, and it does not sell personal information.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
